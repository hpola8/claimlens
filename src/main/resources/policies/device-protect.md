# DeviceProtect Extended Warranty (Sample)
policyType: DEVICE
effectiveDate: 2026-01-01

## DEV-1.1 Accidental Damage from Handling
We cover accidental damage from handling, including drops, cracked screens and liquid spills, on the covered device.

## DEV-1.2 Mechanical and Electrical Failure
We cover failures of the device caused by defects in materials or workmanship after the manufacturer's warranty expires, including battery capacity below 80% of original.

## DEV-2.1 Claim Limit
A maximum of 2 accidental damage claims are covered in any rolling 12-month period.

## DEV-2.2 Service Fees
A service fee of $29 applies to screen-only repairs. A service fee of $99 applies to all other accidental damage repairs or replacements.

## DEV-3.1 Exclusion: Loss and Theft
Loss or theft of the device is not covered under this plan.

## DEV-3.2 Exclusion: Cosmetic Damage
Cosmetic damage such as scratches or dents that do not affect the function of the device is not covered.

## DEV-3.3 Exclusion: Intentional Damage
Damage caused intentionally, or through abuse or unauthorized modification, is not covered.

## DEV-4.1 Proof of Purchase
A proof of purchase showing the device serial number or IMEI is required for every claim.
