# Personal Auto Policy PAP (Sample)
policyType: AUTO
effectiveDate: 2026-01-01

## AUTO-1.1 Collision Coverage
We cover direct and accidental damage to the insured vehicle caused by collision with another vehicle or object, or by upset of the vehicle, regardless of fault.

## AUTO-1.2 Collision Deductible
A deductible of $500 applies to each collision claim.

## AUTO-1.3 Glass Coverage
Repair or replacement of the windshield and other vehicle glass is covered with no deductible.

## AUTO-2.1 Exclusion: Commercial and Rideshare Use
We do not cover any loss that occurs while the vehicle is being used to carry persons or property for a fee, including while the driver is logged into a rideshare or delivery app, unless a rideshare endorsement is listed on the declarations page.

## AUTO-2.2 Exclusion: Unlisted Regular Drivers
We do not cover loss while the vehicle is operated by a person who lives in the insured's household but is not listed as a driver on the policy.

## AUTO-3.1 Hit-and-Run Requirements
For damage caused by an unidentified driver, a police report must be filed within 24 hours of the accident.

## AUTO-4.1 Rental Reimbursement
While the insured vehicle is being repaired for a covered loss, we reimburse rental car expenses up to $40 per day for a maximum of 30 days.

## AUTO-5.1 Total Loss
If repair costs exceed 75% of the vehicle's actual cash value, the vehicle is treated as a total loss and we pay actual cash value minus the deductible.
