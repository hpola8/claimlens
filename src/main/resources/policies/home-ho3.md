# Homeowners Policy HO-3 (Sample)
policyType: HOME
effectiveDate: 2026-01-01

## HOME-1.1 Covered Peril: Sudden and Accidental Water Discharge
We cover direct physical damage to the dwelling and personal property caused by a sudden and accidental discharge of water from a plumbing, heating, air-conditioning or household appliance system, including a burst pipe or failed supply line. Damage to the system or appliance itself is not covered.

## HOME-1.2 Exclusion: Gradual Leaks and Seepage
We do not cover damage caused by continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water that occurs over a period of 14 days or more, whether hidden or visible. This includes slow drips beneath sinks, around toilets and from shower pans.

## HOME-2.1 Covered Peril: Wind and Hail
We cover direct physical damage to the roof, siding and windows caused by windstorm or hail. Interior damage from rain is covered only if wind or hail first creates an opening in the roof or walls.

## HOME-2.3 Exclusion: Wear, Tear and Deterioration
We do not cover wear and tear, marring, deterioration, rust, corrosion, mechanical breakdown, or latent defect. A roof that fails because of age without a covered storm event is excluded.

## HOME-3.1 Covered Peril: Theft
We cover theft of personal property from the residence premises. A police report must be filed within 72 hours of discovering the theft, and the report number must be provided with the claim.

## HOME-4.1 Deductible
A deductible of $1,000 applies to each covered loss. We pay the amount of covered loss that exceeds the deductible.

## HOME-4.2 Large Loss Inspection
Any claim with an estimated loss above $25,000 requires an in-person inspection by a licensed adjuster before payment is authorized.

## HOME-5.1 Limited Mold Coverage
Mold, fungus or wet rot is excluded unless it results directly from a covered sudden water discharge under HOME-1.1. In that case remediation is covered up to a limit of $5,000 per policy term.

## HOME-6.1 Duty to Report
The insured must report a loss to us within 60 days of the date the loss occurred or was discovered. Late reporting may result in denial if it prejudices our ability to investigate.

## HOME-6.2 Proof of Loss
For personal property claims over $2,500, the insured must provide receipts, photographs or other reasonable proof of ownership and value.
